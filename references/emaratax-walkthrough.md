# EmaraTax Corporate Tax registration: field-by-field walkthrough

This is the full path through the form, in order, as of 2026. Screens change, so treat labels as a guide and read what is actually on screen. The user must be logged into EmaraTax before you start.

## Set up the Taxable Person

1. After login, the portal may land on "Other Services." Switch to the Taxable Person context with the "User Type" control in the top bar, and on the User Profile page tick "Taxable Person," then Proceed.
2. If no Taxable Person profile exists, the Home page shows "Create New Taxable Person Profile." Fill:
   - Profile Name in English: the company legal name.
   - Profile Name in Arabic: the Arabic legal name from the licence.
   - Preferred Language: English.
   - Preferred Communication Channel: Email.
   - Click Create.
3. In the Taxable Person list, click View on the new profile to open its dashboard.
4. On the dashboard, in the Registration Overview table, find the Corporate Tax row, click the Action menu (the three dots), and choose Register.
5. The intro page shows sections, time, and "Free of charge." Scroll down, tick "I confirm that I have read the above instructions and guidelines," and click Start.

## Step 1: Entity Details

- **Entity Type:** for a UAE-incorporated company, "Legal Person - Incorporated." (Non-UAE parent would be "Foreign Business.")
- **Entity Sub-Type:** a free zone FZCO or a normal LLC is "UAE Private Company (incl. an Establishment)."
- **Country of Registration:** United Arab Emirates (pre-filled).
- **Date of Incorporation:** from the certificate, DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Are you a Qualifying Public Benefit Entity:** No, for a normal company.
- **Upload Certificate of Incorporation / Memorandum of Association:** upload the certificate and the MOA here (PDF). Upload one file at a time and verify the count (see gotchas).
- **Corporate Tax Period Details:** choose the Corporate Tax Period, which is the months your financial year runs. A calendar-year company is "January - December." The portal then auto-fills, read-only:
  - First Corporate Tax Period Start and End dates.
  - First Corporate Tax Return Filing Due Date. Read this back to the user; it is when their first return is due (typically 9 months after the first period end).
- Next Step.

## Step 2: Identification Details

- **Upload Trade License** (PDF).
- **Trade License Issuing Authority:** searchable dropdown. IFZA / Dubai Silicon Oasis companies: search "Dubai Silicon Oasis" or the exact authority on the licence. (Note: the FTA may re-map this at approval, for example to "Dubai Silicon Oasis." That is normal.)
- **Trade License Number**, **Issue Date**, **Expiry Date.**
- **Legal Name in English** and **in Arabic.**
- **Trade Name** in English and Arabic (optional).
- **Business Activities Details:** click "Add Business Activity," search the activity name from the licence, pick the match (the portal fills industry, group, and the activity code), click Add. Repeat for each activity. Then select one row as the Primary Activity.
- **Do any of the owners hold 25% or more of the ownership?** If yes, the Owners List appears. Click "Add Owner":
  - Owner Type: Natural Person (for an individual).
  - UAE Pass retrieval: No (unless the user wants to authenticate with UAE Pass on their phone).
  - Does the owner hold a valid Emirates ID: Yes.
  - Emirates ID Number and Expiry Date, then click Validate. The portal confirms and pulls the official name.
  - Shareholding Percentage.
  - Add.
- **Local Branch Details:** "Do you have branches in the UAE related to the Trade License": No, unless applicable.
- Next Step.

## Step 3: Contact Details

- **Country:** United Arab Emirates. Selecting UAE switches the address block to UAE fields (Building Name & Number, Street, Area, City, Emirate). Fill from the licence address.
- **Emirate:** dropdown (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, etc.).
- **Mobile: Country Code** +971 and the **Mobile Number** (digits without the leading zero or country code).
- **Landline: Country Code and Number.** This is mandatory. If there is no landline, reuse the mobile digits (see gotchas).
- **Email ID:** the correspondence email.
- **P.O. Box:** optional.
- Next Step.

## Step 4: Authorized Signatory

- Click "Add Authorized Signatory."
- UAE Pass retrieval: No.
- Does the signatory hold a valid Emirates ID: Yes. Enter Emirates ID and Expiry, click Validate.
- Country Code +971, Mobile Number, Email ID, Designation (for example General Manager).
- Source of Authorization: for a GM named in the MOA, choose "Memorandum of Association." (The other option is Power of Attorney.)
- Upload the MOA (PDF) in the section that appears.
- Add.
- Next Step.

## Step 5: Review and Declaration

- The page lists every section with Edit buttons. Check each against the source documents.
- There is a declaration to accept and a Submit button.
- **Hand off here.** The user reads the review, ticks the declaration, and clicks Submit. Do not submit for them.
- After submission, the confirmation screen shows the reference number and submission time. Record them.

## Tips

- Click Save as Draft at the end of each step, or before pausing. Drafts resume from the dashboard.
- The portal auto-fills some dates (like the tax period). Trust the auto-fill over manual entry for those.
- If you paused and came back, open the in-progress application from the Corporate Tax row's Action menu on the Taxable Person dashboard.
