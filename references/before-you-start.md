# Before you start: documents and information checklist

Get these ready before you point the agent at EmaraTax. Having them to hand is the difference between a 40-minute filing and a stop-start hour.

## Account

- An EmaraTax account at eservices.tax.gov.ae, and you logged into it. Free to create with an email and a UAE mobile number. The agent will not log in for you.

## Documents (PDF only)

EmaraTax rejects images (JPG/PNG) on most upload fields. Convert photos of your Emirates ID and passport to PDF first. Max file size is 15 to 20 MB per field.

- **Trade licence** (the main licence, including any branch licences on it).
- **Certificate of incorporation / formation.**
- **Memorandum of Association (MOA) / Articles.** Used twice: as proof of the entity, and as the authorised signatory's proof of authorisation.
- **Emirates ID** of each owner holding 25% or more, and of the authorised signatory (front and back, in date).
- **Passport** of each owner and the authorised signatory (in date).

## Company information

- Legal name in English and Arabic (both are on the trade licence).
- Trade name, if different.
- Licence number.
- Licence issuing authority (for IFZA / Dubai Silicon Oasis companies this is "Dubai Silicon Oasis"; the portal has a searchable list).
- Date of incorporation (from the certificate).
- Licence issue date and expiry date.
- Entity type and sub-type (a UAE free zone FZCO is "Legal Person - Incorporated" then "UAE Private Company").
- Business activities as they appear on the licence. The portal searches by activity name and fills the official code.
- Financial year (most companies are the calendar year, January to December).

## People

For each owner with 25%+ and for the authorised signatory:

- Full name (the portal pulls the official version from the Emirates ID, so it does not have to be typed).
- Emirates ID number (format 784-XXXX-XXXXXXX-X) and its expiry date.
- Shareholding percentage (owners).
- Designation (signatory, for example General Manager).
- The source of the signatory's authority (usually the MOA).

## Contact

- Registered address, matching the trade licence (building, area, emirate).
- A UAE mobile number.
- An email for FTA correspondence. This is where your approval, TRN, and any requests go, so use one you check.

## Good to know before you file

- New companies must register within 3 months of incorporation. The late-registration penalty is AED 10,000.
- Registration is free on EmaraTax. Approval is usually 5 to 20 business days.
- Registering is not the same as paying tax or filing a return. Your first return is due later; the portal shows you the exact date during Step 1.
- If you are unsure about reliefs, VAT, or your tax position, that is a question for a registered UAE tax agent, not for this tool.
